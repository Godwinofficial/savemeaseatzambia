# SaveMeASeat Zambia

### Digital Invitations and Event Management Platform

SaveMeASeat Zambia is a digital event invitation and guest management platform developed by **Godwin Banda**.

The platform provides a modern alternative to traditional paper invitations by allowing users to create, share, and manage digital invitations while giving event organisers tools for RSVP management and guest verification.

**Live Platform:** https://savemeaseatzambia.com

---

## About the Project

SaveMeASeat was developed to make event invitations more convenient, interactive, and accessible.

The platform is designed around the complete guest journey, from receiving an invitation and submitting an RSVP to being approved and verified at the event.

The project initially focused on digital wedding invitations and has evolved toward a broader event management platform.

---

## Core Features

### Digital Event Invitations

Interactive invitations can include:

* Event details
* Date and time
* Venue information
* Event story
* Photo galleries
* Gift information
* Mobile money details
* Google Maps location
* Custom sections
* Animated elements

### RSVP Management

Guests can respond directly through their digital invitation.

Event organisers can:

* View RSVP submissions
* Approve guests
* Decline guests
* Monitor responses
* Manage approved guests

### Digital Guest Cards

Approved guests can receive a digital guest card containing their name and QR code.

The guest card provides a convenient way to identify approved guests during event entry.

### QR Guest Verification

QR codes can be scanned during event entry to verify approved guests and simplify the check in process.

### Event Management

The platform is being developed toward a broader event management system where users can create and manage their own events and invitations.
